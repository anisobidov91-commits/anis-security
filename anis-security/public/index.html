Anis Security

Yordamchii amniyat: fayl, havola va parolro dar telefon tekshiri kuned.

Tekshiri fayl

Fayl dar telefoni shumo mondaast: ba hech ja firistoda nameshavad.

Tekshiri havola

Havola kushoda nameshavad va ba hech ja firistoda nameshavad. Faqat shakli on tahlil meshavad.

Tekshiri parol

Parol faqat dar hamin sahifa tahlil meshavad. Hech ja firistoda va nigoh doshta nameshavad. Agar shak dored, paroli vokeii hisobhoyatonro inja nanavised.

Dar borai Anis Security

Kor mekunad: hashi fayl, alomathoi havolai mashkuk, quvvati parol. Hama dar dastgohi shumo, bedun internet.

Namekunad: virusro mustaqilona nest namekunad va hakerro namebandad. Natija faqat ogohi ast, na kafolat.

Navbati: tekshiri sizdoho (parol), skaneri headerhoi sayt va bazai virusho. Baroi in server ya kaliti API lozim ast, pas hanuz ulanmaast.

Maslihat: navsozii muntazam, tasdiqi du-marhala (2FA), nasbi barnomaho faqat az Google Play, nusxai zahira.
